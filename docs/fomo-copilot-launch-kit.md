# FOMO Copilot Submission Kit

## One-Line Positioning

`Miraix FOMO Copilot` lets a user say "I have 100U, what should I trade today?", see a free setup, pay with `fxUSD` or `USDC`, and then execute the full trade path with `OKX OnchainOS`.

## Core Story

- `Miraix` handles intent, packaging, and strategy framing
- `OKX OnchainOS` provides live market data, quotes, routes, and execution payloads
- `fxUSD on Base` works as the payment rail for premium agent actions

One sentence:

`Miraix strategy -> OKX execution -> fxUSD settlement`

## 30-Second Demo Script

1. Open `/fomo-copilot`
2. Say:
   `I have 100 USDC. What should I trade today?`
3. Choose:
   - budget
   - risk
   - horizon
4. Click `生成免费预览`
5. Highlight:
   - theme
   - FOMO score
   - three trade legs
6. Click `支付 0.05 fxUSD 并执行` or `支付 0.05 USDC 并执行`
7. Confirm payment
8. Show the success state:
   - payment accepted
   - all legs broadcast
   - share image ready
9. End on the result image
